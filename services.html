Complete Logistics Solutions

From residential relocation to worldwide freight, we offer a full range of logistics and relocation services tailored to your needs.

Moving to a new home should be an exciting experience, not a stressful one. At MS Logistics, we provide professional residential relocation services that ensure your household belongings are packed, transported, and delivered safely.

International Household Relocation

Relocating overseas requires careful planning and expert coordination. MS Logistics provides complete international household relocation services, managing everything from export-quality packing and freight arrangements to customs documentation support.

International Global Relocation

Embassy & Diplomatic Relocation

MS Logistics specialises in relocation services for embassies, diplomatic missions, international organisations, and expatriate professionals. We understand the importance of confidentiality, precision, and timely execution.

Diplomatic Confidential Handling

Corporate & Office Relocation

We help businesses relocate efficiently with minimal disruption to daily operations. From offices and corporate headquarters to institutional facilities, our team carefully plans and manages every aspect of the move.

Corporate Office Relocation

Air Freight Services

Our air freight services are ideal for clients who require fast, secure, and reliable international transportation of household goods. Every shipment is professionally packed, documented, and coordinated.

Freight Air Cargo

Sea Freight Services

Sea freight is a cost-effective solution for medium and large international household shipments. We provide complete sea freight management, including export packing, container booking, and documentation support.

Freight Sea Cargo

Customs Documentation & Clearance Support

International relocations involve complex documentation and customs procedures. Our experienced team assists with preparing the required paperwork, coordinating export formalities, and supporting customs clearance.

Customs Clearance Support

Secure Warehousing & Storage

MS Logistics offers secure short-term and long-term warehousing solutions for household goods, office equipment, and export shipments. Our warehouse is designed to keep your belongings safe, organised, and protected.
