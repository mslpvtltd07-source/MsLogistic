Kathmandu based. Internationally routed.

MS Logistics Pvt. Ltd. is a Kathmandu-based household goods packing, transportation, and relocation company. We specialize in providing complete relocation solutions for Embassies, Diplomatic Missions, International Organizations, INGOs, corporate offices, and individual clients.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected logistics and international relocation service providers in Nepal by delivering professional, reliable and customer-focused solution for diplomatic missions, international organizations, corporate clients and individuals.

Our Mission

To provide complete household goods packing, transportation and international relocation services through professional planning, high-quality packing standards and efficient logistics coordination.

Our Promise: Absolute accountability, single-point responsibility, and a zero-damage standard on every shipment, from a single household move to institutional cargo.
